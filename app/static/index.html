How to use this tutor

  1. Ask about a topic. Type a question like "explain the chain rule" for a full explanation.
  2. Stuck on a problem? Ask for help with it directly. You'll get a hint, not the answer - ask again after each attempt and the hints get more specific.
  3. Get your work checked. Tick "this is work to check", type the problem in the field that appears, then your working in the message box below it - two separate fields, so it grades against the right problem.
  4. Or just photograph it. With "this is work to check" ticked, use "attach photo" instead of typing your working - a picture of your handwritten steps works too.
  5. Ready for something harder? Once you've shown you've got a topic down, ask to be challenged and you'll get a tougher problem to try instead of a repeat.
  6. Your progress carries over. The student/session codes in the top right identify you between visits - keep them the same (they're saved automatically) to pick up where you left off.
